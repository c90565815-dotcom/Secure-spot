03 / KNOW THE RISKS

Common Cyber Threats

01

Phishing

Fake emails, messages, or websites designed to steal sensitive information.

02

Malware

Malicious software that can damage devices, steal data, or compromise systems.

03

Social Engineering

Manipulation that tricks people into revealing information or granting access.

04

Weak Passwords

Easily guessed or reused passwords that make accounts vulnerable.

05

Public Wi-Fi Risks

Untrusted networks that may expose sensitive activity or information.

04 / STAY PROTECTED

Essential Security Practices

01. Verify the sender and destination before opening links.

02. Use strong, unique passwords for every account.

03. Enable two-factor authentication whenever possible.

04. Avoid sensitive transactions on untrusted networks.

05. Keep your operating system and applications updated.

06. Never share passwords or verification codes.
